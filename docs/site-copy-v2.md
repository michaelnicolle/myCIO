## Decisions this copy rests on

Taken in the A/B session on 26 October 2026. Every section was then approved line by line; the only change was the Who we are lead, which now combines the why with the founders line.

1. Three situations replace the two doors as the entrance. journey.html folds into Home.
2. The gap keeps the table and "We take that seat." The costs and the manifesto go; the costs live inside the three situations.
3. The Climb leaves Home except for one line. It becomes the Fractional CIO graphic and is explained there.
4. Four practices on Home are name plus one line. Detail lives on What we do.
5. Hero lead is one line, no list of practices.
6. The ownership list is cut to one line in the proof section and one closing line on What we do.
7. Proof is the $500k and the question. No anonymised outcomes until there is a real one with a number.
8. Four pages: Home, What we do and outcomes, Who we are, Contact. Practice pages, How we work, The journey and The case go.
9. One or two outcomes exist today, so they sit under the practice that produced them, not on a page of their own.
10. The ask is the call. "Pick one thing" is the promise beside the form, not a second button.
11. Form is three fields: name, email, one open question.
12. Budgets: Home 450, What we do 300, Who we are 300, Contact 150.
