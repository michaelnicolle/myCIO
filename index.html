A few straight answers.

Is myCIO a replacement for our IT provider?

We can work with your existing provider, your internal team, or a mix. Our core role is independent technology leadership: setting direction, holding delivery to account and connecting the work to your business priorities.

Do we need a big transformation programme?

No. Start with the problem worth solving. That might be a vendor review, a stalled project or a workflow that costs your people time. The scope follows the need.

Do we need to be ready for AI?

You need a useful problem to solve. We assess your data, access controls and ways of working first. Sometimes AI fits. Sometimes a simpler process or better use of your existing tools is the right answer.
