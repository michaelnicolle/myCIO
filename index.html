A few straight answers.

Is myCIO a replacement for our IT provider?

We can work with your existing provider, your internal team, or a mix. Our core role is independent technology leadership: setting direction, holding delivery to account and connecting the work to your business priorities.

Do we need a big transformation programme?

No. Start with a conversation about your business and what you would like to improve. We help you find the opportunities, decide what matters most and choose a practical first step.

Do we need to be ready for AI?

No. You just need to be looking for ways to improve your business. You do not need to know where AI fits or have a specific problem worked out. We explore the opportunities with you and help turn the right ones into practical improvements for your people.

Where could your
business go next?

Thirty minutes with a founder. Tell us about your business and what you would like to improve. We will explore the possibilities together and help you see a useful next step.
