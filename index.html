Who we work with

Leaders who know what they want. And leaders who need the words.

You have the plan

More on the roadmap than the team can carry.

You have a clear view of where the business is going and what the technology has to do to get there. What you are short of is senior capacity beside you: people who have done it before, will hold the vendors to account, and get it over the line without it becoming another thing you manage.

Usually an IT Manager, CIO or General Manager

You have the ambition

You know something has to change. Nobody has put it into words.

The business has outgrown the way its technology is run. Proposals arrive full of brand names and thin on outcomes, and the AI conversation never ships anything. You need someone to turn the ambition into a direction, outcomes the board can fund, and a plan with an owner.

Usually a CFO, COO, owner or board member

Either way, the first conversation is about the business, not the IT.

The gap

Three things happen in every business. Two of them have an owner.

That third seat is the one we take. Not to replace the people you have. To give them a direction worth delivering.
